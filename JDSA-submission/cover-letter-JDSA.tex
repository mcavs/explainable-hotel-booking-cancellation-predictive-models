Dear Editor,

I am pleased to submit our research manuscript entitled \textbf{``Explainable Hotel Booking Cancellation Prediction Models: Towards Trustworthy Survival Analysis in Hospitality''} for consideration for publication in the \textit{International Journal of Data Science and Analytics}.

Hotel booking cancellation is a high-stakes operational problem at the intersection of data science, machine learning, and business decision-making. Its traditional formulation as a static binary classification task conceals critical temporal structure and introduces systematic bias by mishandling right-censored active reservations. This study addresses these deficiencies by recasting cancellation prediction as a continuous time-to-event (also called survival analysis) problem and embedding it within an end-to-end explainable artificial intelligence framework. The manuscript delivers three primary contributions:

\begin{enumerate}
    \setlength{\itemsep}{0pt}
    \setlength{\parskip}{1pt}
    \item We benchmark 10 survival algorithms across two structurally distinct real-world hotel booking datasets, demonstrating that while standard Random Survival Forests maximize discriminative ranking ($C$-index), Oblique Random Survival Forests and Conditional Survival Forests achieve superior probability calibration---a property indispensable for revenue management decisions such as dynamic overbooking quota configuration and cancellation fee scheduling.
    \item We introduce Survival Permutation Feature Importance (SurvPFI) and Partial Dependence Survival Profiles (SurvPDP) to the hospitality domain. These time-dependent XAI tools track the evolving marginal impact of covariates across the full booking lifecycle, uncovering compounding price elasticity near check-in and detecting policy-driven structural anomalies invisible to static classifiers.
    \item We translate algorithmic outputs into actionable operational intelligence, demonstrating how auditable survival curves support dynamic overbooking quotas, optimized re-marketing windows, and responsible AI governance in hospitality revenue management.
\end{enumerate}

This work aligns directly with several of JDSA's primary topical areas. First, it advances the \textbf{statistical and mathematical foundations} of survival analysis by extending permutation-based feature importance and partial dependence methods to the time-to-event setting. Second, it contributes to \textbf{data analytics, pattern recognition, and knowledge discovery} through a rigorous multi-algorithm benchmarking study on large-scale real-world transactional data. Third, it addresses \textbf{active, real-time, personalized, and actionable analytics} by equipping practitioners with continuously updated, individual-level cancellation hazard estimates that enable targeted retention interventions. Fourth, and importantly, it directly engages with JDSA's scope on \textbf{ethics, trust, and responsible data science}: the SurvPFI and SurvPDP frameworks produce auditable, human-interpretable explanations that operationalize trustworthy AI principles within a high-stakes business domain. Finally, the study represents a significant \textbf{data science application in the business domain}, demonstrating how survival modeling transforms raw transactional booking records into strategic decision intelligence for the global hospitality industry.\\
