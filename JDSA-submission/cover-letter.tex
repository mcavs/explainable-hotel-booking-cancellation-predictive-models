\textbf{Subject:} Submission of Original Research Article for Special Issue Consideration

Dear Guest Editors and Editorial Board,

I am pleased to submit our original research manuscript entitled \textbf{``Explainable Hotel Booking Cancellation Prediction Models: Towards Trustworthy Survival Analysis in Hospitality''} for consideration for publication in \textit{Electronic Markets} within the Special Issue on \textbf{``Responsible and Trustworthy Artificial Intelligence in Tourism and Hospitality''}.

As predictive algorithms increasingly govern hotel revenue management, reliance on opaque ``black-box'' systems creates acute vulnerabilities regarding operational stability, customer trust, and algorithmic accountability. Traditional hospitality literature frames cancellation forecasting as a static binary classification problem, which ignores \textit{when} attrition events occur and introduces systematic predictive bias by treating active, right-censored bookings as non-canceled. To operationalize the core tenets of Responsible and Trustworthy AI, this study introduces an end-to-end explainable time-to-event (survival analysis) framework delivering three primary contributions:
\begin{enumerate}
    \setlength{\itemsep}{0pt}
    \setlength{\parskip}{1pt}
    \item We reframe hotel cancellation prediction as a continuous time-to-event problem, benchmarking 10 survival algorithms across two structurally distinct datasets. We demonstrate that while tree ensembles maximize discriminative ranking, Oblique Random Survival Forests and Conditional Survival Forests achieve superior probability calibration, which is critical for revenue management.
    \item We introduce Survival Permutation Feature Importance and Partial Dependence Survival Profiles to hospitality. These methods track the evolving marginal impact of covariates across the booking lifecycle, empirically unmasking compounding price elasticity near check-in and detecting policy-driven structural anomalies.
    \item We bridge algorithmic outputs with operational practice, demonstrating how auditable survival curves enable dynamic overbooking quotas, optimal re-marketing windows, and trustworthy algorithmic governance.
\end{enumerate}
